# Roadmap

Driftfin is alpha. This is where it is heading, not a schedule - plans change as real use shows what matters.

## Being worked on now

- Playback: pause-screen responsiveness on TV hardware.
- Subtitles: picture subtitles (PGS, VobSub) are not shown today; options for handling them are being investigated.

## Planned

- Auto quality: lower the bitrate automatically when the connection cannot keep up, instead of just buffering, on TV and phone.
- Subtitles: better ASS/SSA positioning and styling.
- Player tuning: ExoPlayer buffer and track-selection settings for smoother playback on TV hardware.
- A better on-screen keyboard for TV search.
- "Pick for me" in the Home banner row.
- Continued TV/phone parity: the same features working the same way on both.

## Ideas, not decided

- A self-hosted browser version with a layout closer to the TV app.
- Distributing the phone app through Google Play.

## How to help

- Real-device testing is the biggest gap: the app is developed on one Sony Google TV, and other Android TV / Google TV devices and the Fire Stick 4K Max still need hands-on time. Bug reports with your device model and what you were doing when it went wrong are the most useful thing right now.
- Feature suggestions are welcome; open an issue to discuss before building something big.
