My Stuff

Favorites and Collections come from Jellyfin; the Watchlist is Driftfin's own list, stored with your sign-in here. Watched status and Continue Watching live in Jellyfin, so they follow you between devices.

Settings

Saved server-side, per Jellyfin user, so they follow you between devices.

Profile

Display only, saved with your Driftfin settings on this server. Your Jellyfin username and account picture are not changed.

Updates

Checking compares the code running here with the latest browser code on GitHub. Nothing is downloaded or applied - recreating your container is what updates it.

Diagnostics

Temporary on-screen log of player taps and fullscreen state for troubleshooting. Nothing is saved or sent. Toggling off clears it; a reload also clears it unless the page was opened with ?playerdiag=1 in the address.

Account

Switch user signs you out and returns to sign-in, where another Jellyfin user can sign in with a password or Quick Connect. No credentials are stored.
